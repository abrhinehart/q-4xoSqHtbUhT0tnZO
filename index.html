Five Words

Tuesday
25 August 2026

Five new ones. The sentence has to carry the word. The drawing is only the main clause.

Diagram: subject | verb | object. A backslash after the verb is a predicate adjective. Words hanging off the line are modifiers.

01

germane

adj · Latin germanus, “of the same stock” · relevant sense 1600s

Closely tied to the matter at hand. Not a cousin of the topic. The topic.

The question about parking was not germane to the budget vote, and the chair cut it off.

02

jaded

adj · from jade, a worn-out horse · 1630s

Worn down by too much of the same thing. Bored, and a little hard.

After ten years of the same pitch, she sounded jaded, as if no deal could surprise her.

03

inure

verb · Old French oeuvre, “work” · 1400s

Harden by use. You get used to it. You do not have to like it.

The night shifts inured him to fatigue, though they never made the work pleasant.

04

caustic

adj · Greek kaiein, “to burn” · 1400s

It burns. Of speech: sarcastic enough to scorch.

His caustic remark about the slides landed, and the room went quiet for a full minute.

05

dilatory

adj · Latin differe, “to put off” · 1400s

Slow on purpose. Delay as a tactic, not as an accident.

The committee was dilatory on purpose, hoping the deadline would pass before anyone voted.

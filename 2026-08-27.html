Five Words

Thursday
27 August 2026

Five new ones. The sentence has to carry the word. Tap Hear to say it out loud.

01

garrulous

/ˈɡɛɹ.ə.ləs/

adj · Latin garrulus, “talkative” · 1610s

Too ready to talk. Not just chatty: the extra story keeps coming.

The garrulous driver narrated every exit from Tampa to Winter Haven, and nobody in the back seat had the heart to stop him.

02

sanguine

/ˈsæŋ.ɡwɪn/

adj · Latin sanguis, “blood” · c. 1500

Cheerfully sure it will work out. Old medicine blamed an excess of blood for that mood.

He stayed sanguine about the refinance even after the second rate lock expired on a Friday.

03

intransigent

/ɪnˈtɹæn.sə.d͡ʒənt/

adj · Spanish intransigentes, “those not coming to agreement” · 1874

Will not yield. Not quiet. Unwilling to meet you in the middle.

The board was intransigent on the 7 a.m. start, so the rest of the agenda never got a vote.

04

quotidian

/kwoʊˈtɪdiən/

adj · Latin quotidianus, “daily” · mid-14c

Ordinary because it returns every day. Small until it isn’t.

The leak was a quotidian drip under the sink until the cabinet floor finally gave way.

05

abrogate

/ˈæb.ɹəˌɡeɪt/

verb · Latin abrogare, “to repeal” · 1520s

Cancel it by authority. Not ignore it. Kill the rule on purpose.

One signature would abrogate the old noncompete, and she could start on Monday without a lawyer in the room.

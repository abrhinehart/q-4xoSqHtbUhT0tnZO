Policy

Clarity cloture fails 49–50

By a vote of 49–50 on Tuesday Sep 15, the Senate did not agree to invoke cloture on the motion to proceed to H.R. 3633 (Digital Asset Market Clarity Act) — well short of the 60 votes needed to open floor debate. Senator Coons did not vote; Sens. Collins, Hawley, Moran, and Tillis voted no, with Tillis casting no specifically to preserve a motion to reconsider (entered at 3:01 p.m. ET). Sen. Lummis framed the loss as Democrats choosing politics; Majority Leader Thune had cast regulatory certainty as key to U.S. digital-asset leadership earlier that day. The procedural loss stalls market-structure legislation in the 2026 window; Tillis’s reconsideration keeps a thin path open but does not restart debate on its own.

Bridge

Liquid pauses after ~3,996 BTC peg-out

Blockstream’s Liquid Network paused after roughly 3,996 BTC (~$320m) left the federation peg wallet Sunday through a SideSwap Peg-out Authorization Key. Liquid says the PAK and other keys were not compromised; Blockstream and SideSwap blame a bug in the Elements node software. Reserves fell from about 4,200 BTC to ~200 BTC; bridge nodes disabled; L-BTC deposits and withdrawals paused at exchanges. Other Liquid assets (USDT, RWAs) called unaffected. The recipient labeled itself “whitehats” on-chain and is negotiating return of most coins after a patch; no confirmed return as of this morning. Sector heat may mint memes — Froth lane if anything.

Clarity Act cloture Sep 15

Senate cloture on the motion to proceed to H.R. 3633 (Digital Asset Market Clarity Act) remains set for Tuesday, Sep 15 at 2:15 p.m. EDT — 60 votes to open floor debate, not final passage. Majority Leader John Thune filed the motion; Republicans hold 53 seats, so roughly seven Democratic or independent votes are still required. Ethics language, illicit-finance reach, and DeFi/stablecoin fights remain the named blockers. Same week as FOMC.

Peptides

Gan & Lee × Menarini — bofanglutide Europe

Gan & Lee and Menarini announced Sep 3 an exclusive Europe license for bofanglutide (GZR18), an investigational once-every-two-weeks subcutaneous GLP-1 RA for adults with obesity or overweight. Economics: €62m upfront, up to €664m milestones, double-digit royalties (headline up to €726m excluding royalties). Menarini handles regulatory submissions and commercialization across 39 countries. Still investigational — not EMA-authorized; global Phase 3 intended for European registration. Same week: NMPA cleared clinical trials of GZC8072 Tablet, a once-weekly oral peptide GLP-1 RA for type 2 diabetes.

Biomed

Hengrui oral GLP-1 filings accepted

Hengrui (via Shandong Shengdi) said NMPA accepted marketing applications for HRS-7535 tablets, an oral once-daily small-molecule GLP-1R agonist: type 2 diabetes (Sep 2) and long-term adult weight management (Sep 3). Not an approval — acceptance starts review. Weight file rests on HARBOR-1 (n=556): Hengrui reports 180 mg mean loss 10.9% at week 44 and 11.1% at week 50 vs ~2.5–2.6% placebo. Ex-Greater China rights sit with Kailera as KAI-7535.

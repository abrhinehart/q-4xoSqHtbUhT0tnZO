Infrastructure

Liquid emergency Elements patch

Liquid said Sep 9 that Elements v23.3.4 is available, functionary nodes are upgrading, and node operators should follow — the release hardens range-proof verification cache keys after the Sep 6 unbacked L-BTC mint/peg-out. Reviews included Bitcoin Red Team and Alpen Labs. Staged recovery: (1) resume block production with peg ops still suspended, (2) replay verified-valid transactions, (3) restore pegs only after state and fund-return confirmation. ~598.5 BTC of the ~4,000 BTC peg-out is still outstanding. Samson Mow’s warning stands — do not send BTC to peg-in addresses until restart is confirmed. Fake update sites are circulating; official Liquid/Blockstream channels only.

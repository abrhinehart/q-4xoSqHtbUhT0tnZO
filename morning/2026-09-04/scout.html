Biggest ETF day since January

Spot bitcoin ETFs posted their biggest single-day haul since mid-January: Farside shows Sep 3 net +$730.8m after Wednesday’s +$101.1m, with BlackRock IBIT +$454.0m (~62% of the buy), ARKB +$137.7m, and FBTC +$74.4m; only HODL and BTCW printed outflows. Payrolls into FOMC

August nonfarm payrolls land this morning (8:30 a.m. ET) — last major labor print before the Sep 15–16 FOMC. CME FedWatch swung from roughly 60–70% odds of a 25 bp hike earlier this week toward a coin-flip into the print (hold at 3.50–3.75% vs lift to 3.75–4.00%), after Chair Kevin Warsh’s Jackson Hole hawkishness and more recent softer pushback from Fed Governor Christopher Waller. Soft jobs can cut hike odds; August CPI on Sep 11 can put them right back.

Clarity Act cloture

Senate Majority Leader John Thune’s cloture on the motion to proceed to H.R. 3633 (Digital Asset Market Clarity Act) still ripens Tuesday, Sep 15 at 2:15 p.m. EDT — 60 votes to open floor debate, not final passage. Republicans still need roughly seven Democratic or independent votes; ethics language, DeFi liability, and stablecoin-yield fights remain the named blockers. The calendar sits on the same week as FOMC, so a failed cloture risks pushing market-structure work past the election window.

Blockstream rejects Liquid ransom

Cointelegraph’s Ezra Reguerra reported Friday that Blockstream will not pay for the ~598 BTC still held after the Sep 6 exploit — “Taking assets without authorization… is a crime, not responsible disclosure.” Actors had demanded a 10% bounty; ~3,400 of ~4,000 BTC peg-out returned after bridge nodes were patched.

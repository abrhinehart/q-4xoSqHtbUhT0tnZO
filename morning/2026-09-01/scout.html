Robinhood Chain

App revenue second only to Solana

Robinhood Chain’s apps booked about $2.66m of 24h app revenue on August 30 (DefiLlama via CoinDesk), ahead of Ethereum and second only to Solana, on a record 5.52m transactions and roughly $875m DEX volume.

Memecoin launch and trading tools drove most of it; about 22,600 tokens launched that day via Pons. App revenue is protocol fees, not Robinhood corporate revenue. Sector heat shows in the RH meme mcap print above.

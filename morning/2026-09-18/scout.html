SEC Innovation Exemption · tokenized stocks

On Thu Sep 17 the Commission issued an order letting qualifying Tokenized Securities Venues trade tokenized versions of NMS-listed stocks through permissioned AMM/liquidity-pool models without registering as traditional exchanges, and giving qualifying liquidity providers temporary dealer relief. Chair Paul Atkins framed it as an interim bridge that “must be followed by durable rulemaking,” two days after Senate cloture failed on H.R. 3633 (Clarity). Conditions include identical economic and voting rights versus traditional shares, a ban on synthetic stock exposure, issuer notice with a 30-day objection window, public/auditable smart contracts, synchronized trading halts with the primary listing venue, and volume limits.

Reuters · CNBC · H.R. 3633

CFTC · Phantom-style software relief broadened

The same Thursday, Market Participants Division release 9300-26 and Staff Letter 26-25 said staff will not recommend IB or associated-person enforcement against providers of passive trading software (and their personnel), subject to conditions, when the software only facilitates user trades routed to registered FCMs, IBs, and designated contract markets. The position mirrors Staff Letter 26-09 (Phantom) and now makes that path broadly available to qualifying providers who neither custody funds nor exercise trading discretion.

Liquid gets 3,400 BTC back

After Sunday’s ~3,996–4,000 BTC peg-out (~$320m) via SideSwap’s Peg-out Authorization Key path — Liquid said PAK/federation keys were not compromised and blamed an Elements bug — the “white hat” party returned exactly 3,400 BTC to the labeled peg script at 16:09 UTC Sep 7 (tx bc49a46d) and kept ~598.5 BTC (~15%, ~$48m) as an implied bounty. On-chain OP_RETURN traffic with Blockstream covered patching bridge nodes before the return; Liquid’s last official X statement still describes a pause, and L-BTC deposit/withdrawal halts have not been publicly lifted. Sector heat may still mint memes — Froth lane if anything.

Regulation

Clarity Act cloture still Sep 15

Senate cloture on the motion to proceed to H.R. 3633 (Digital Asset Market Clarity Act) remains set for Tuesday, Sep 15 at 2:15 p.m. ET — 60 votes to open floor debate, not final passage. Majority Leader John Thune filed the motion Aug 8; Republicans hold 53 seats, so roughly seven Democratic or independent votes are still required. Ethics language, developer protection, commodities language, and stablecoin/crypto-rewards fights remain the named blockers. Same week as FOMC (Sep 15–16); CPI is Sep 10–11.

Exchange rules

SEC clears Nasdaq Texas commodity trusts

SEC Release No. 34-106268 (Sep 3) granted accelerated approval to Nasdaq Texas Rule 5711(d) for Commodity-Based Trust Shares: a “digital commodity” definition informed by the March 2026 SEC–CFTC joint interpretation, removal of the passive-only constraint, and a 15% NAV sleeve for non-qualifying digital commodities/securities (85% must still qualify). BTC, ETH, SOL, and XRP appear as examples meeting eligibility criteria — that is exchange listing arithmetic, not a new federal commodity classification. The order is substantially identical to July approvals for Nasdaq/NYSE Arca/Cboe; statutory CLARITY language would still supersede the provisional exchange definition.

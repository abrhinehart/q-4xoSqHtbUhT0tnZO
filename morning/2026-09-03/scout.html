Story

Clarity Act cloture

Senate Majority Leader John Thune’s cloture on the motion to proceed to H.R. 3633 (Digital Asset Market Clarity Act) still ripens Tuesday, Sep 15 at 2:15 p.m. EDT — 60 votes to open floor debate, not final passage. Republicans need roughly seven Democratic or independent votes; ethics language, DeFi liability (Section 604), and stablecoin-yield fights remain the named blockers. CFTC Chair Michael Selig has said the agency can take limited crypto-market steps under existing authority if legislation stalls. Separately, Strategy’s Michael Saylor and CEO Phong Le filed an Aug 31 response urging MSCI to withdraw a “non-operating company” index screen (feedback through Sep 30; decision expected by Oct 16).

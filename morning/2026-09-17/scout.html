Fed hike 25 bp

On Wed Sep 16 the FOMC raised the federal funds target range by ¼ percentage point to 3¾–4 percent by a unanimous 12–0 vote — the first adjustment since 2023 — citing elevated inflation and a solid expansion. Chair Kevin Warsh’s press-conference opener said inflation “is too high and has been for too long,” that the Jackson Hole standard had not been met, and that financial conditions were hard to call restrictive. SEP median year-end funds rate 4.1% for both 2026 and 2027. IORB to 3.90% and primary credit to 4.0%, effective Sep 17.

Five Words

Saturday
19 September 2026

Five new ones. The sentence has to carry the word. Tap Hear to say it out loud.

01

affable

/ˈæf.ə.bəl/ · AF-uh-buhl

adj · French affable from Latin affābilis · late 15c

Warmly approachable — friendly, courteous, and easy to talk with.

The new managing partner was so affable that even the vendors who usually braced for a fight left the room smiling.

02

imperious

/ɪmˈpɪɹ.i.əs/ · im-PEER-ee-uhs

adj · Latin imperiōsus from imperium · 1540s

Arrogantly commanding — domineering in manner or tone.

With an imperious flick of the wrist she silenced the table before anyone had finished the objection.

03

blithe

/blaɪð/ · BLYTHE

adj · Old English blīþe “joyful, kind” · OE

Cheerfully unconcerned — casually indifferent, sometimes carelessly so.

His blithe reply to the missed deadline suggested he had never once checked the shared calendar.

04

caustic

/ˈkɔstɪk/ · KAW-stik

adj · Latin causticus from Greek kaustikós · figurative 1771

Sharply sarcastic — biting or scathing in speech.

One caustic aside about the deck’s typos landed harder than the formal critique that followed.

05

vivacious

/vɪˈveɪ.ʃəs/ · vih-VAY-shuhs

adj · Latin vīvāx “lively” + -acious · 1640s

Lively and high-spirited — full of energy in manner or talk.

Her vivacious opening remarks pulled a quiet boardroom into the discussion within two minutes.
